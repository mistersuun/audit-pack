# AD (Analyse Departementale -- F&B Departmental Analysis)

## Overview

The AD sheet is the most comprehensive revenue tracking sheet in the RJ workbook, spanning 60 columns that cover every revenue category in the hotel. It provides a daily breakdown of departmental revenue across all food & beverage outlets, ancillary services, taxes, and room inventory metrics. Each row represents one day of the month, giving a complete 31-day picture of hotel operations at maximum granularity.

This sheet is not directly integrated into the web application but mirrors data from the `jour` sheet while adding room-type mix detail and ancillary revenue breakdowns not captured elsewhere.

## Data Flow & Integration

```
jour sheet (daily revenue) ──> AD (mirrors + expands)
                                 │
                                 ├── F&B department detail (5 outlets x 5 sub-categories)
                                 ├── 18 ancillary revenue categories
                                 ├── 3 tax categories
                                 └── 8 room inventory metrics
```

The AD sheet is a read-only analysis layer. It is not written to by the web application but can be consumed for reporting. Its data originates from the same daily audit process that feeds the `jour` sheet, with additional room-type breakdown columns (SIMPLE through DISPONIBLE) that are unique to this sheet.

## Analytics Potential

- **Daily department performance**: Compare Giotto vs Piazza vs Marche revenue day-over-day.
- **Room type mix analysis**: Track the ratio of single/double/suite/comp rooms over the month.
- **Ancillary revenue trends**: Monitor internet, laundry, minibar, and other non-core revenue streams.
- **Tax reconciliation**: Verify TVQ/TPS/TVH amounts against total revenue.
- **Outlet beverage analysis**: Break down alcohol revenue (beer vs wine vs spirits) by outlet.
- **Occupancy vs revenue correlation**: Cross-reference room inventory columns with revenue columns.
